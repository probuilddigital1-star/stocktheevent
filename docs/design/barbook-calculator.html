Questions

Wine at a wedding

Red or white, and how much of each?

Plan 60/40 red to white for fall and winter and flip it for spring and summer. For 41 bottles that is 25 red and 16 white; buy an extra case of whichever your crowd favors.

Should the champagne toast come out of this number?

No. One flute per guest for the toast is its own line: 17 bottles for 100 guests. Add it on the champagne page for this wedding.

What if the venue charges corkage?

Ask whether unopened bottles can be returned; most retailers take back sealed cases within 30 days, which makes the 15 percent margin nearly free.
