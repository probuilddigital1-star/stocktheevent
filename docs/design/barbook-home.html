How the math works

Every answer shows its work

Start with the guests who drink (70 to 80 percent, fewer at big events). Give each one about a drink and a half in the first hour, then slow the pace as the night goes on. Split the total across the drinks you serve the way that occasion tends to, convert to bottles and cases, and add 15 percent so the last hour is not a scramble.

Every calculator page prints those steps with your numbers in them, so you can change any assumption you disagree with.
